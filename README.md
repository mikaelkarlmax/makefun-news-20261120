**The World in One Page** — a single 1024×1024 broadsheet that sums up the world news of **Friday 20 November 2026**: 46 numbered stories across all 7 regions, each one pinned to a dotted world map, with a UTC timeline, a strip of six big numbers, and an explicit date check. Every story happened, or was first reported, on 20 November 2026 — from Russia's record November barrage on Kyiv's Dnipro bridges and Cyclone Akvan's Odisha landfall, to the World Court's Myanmar genocide ruling and Wall Street's AI-rout slide into correction.

The entry program copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The poster itself is drawn and typeset from `poster.html` (data in `stories.json`), rendered headlessly to produce the shipped image.

## Run it

node main.ts
